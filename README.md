# Softlligence

**Custom software organizations actually run on.**

Softlligence Technologies is a founder-led software company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, operating since May 2024. We build our own business software products and design, engineer and ship custom systems for companies, schools, agencies and nonprofits.

## What we offer

### Products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **ERP** | Brings purchasing, stock, finance and daily operations into one system |
| **CRM** | Manages leads, deals and customer history |
| **MIS** | Turns operations data into dashboards, reports and management summaries |
| **Inventory Management** | Tracks stock levels, movements and reorder points across warehouses |

Our products include AI-powered recommendations and summaries, and can run in the cloud, connect to your existing systems or be deployed on your own servers.

### Ready-made tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Showroom** | A website platform for car brands, distributors and dealerships, with a 360° car viewer |
| **Cleanmeta** | Inspects and removes hidden image metadata, entirely in the browser |
| **Softlligence Voice** | Creates a custom voice from a consented recording and turns text into speech |

### Services

- **Enterprise systems:** ERP, CRM, HRMS, warehouse, supply chain, manufacturing execution and business intelligence
- **Web and SaaS:** websites, web applications, client portals and internal dashboards
- **Mobile apps:** Android, iOS and React Native
- **Artificial intelligence:** AI integration, assistants, document intelligence and automation
- **Product design:** user research, wireframes, prototypes and design systems
- **Cloud, DevOps and support:** AWS and Azure deployment, CI/CD, monitoring and maintenance

## Who we serve

Manufacturing, garments, steel, logistics, retail, healthcare, education, financial services, real estate, government, NGOs and startups.

## How we work

| Stage | Typical duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | 1–2 weeks | Requirements, roadmap and estimate |
| **Design** | 2–4 weeks | A clickable prototype before production code |
| **Build** | Varies by scope | Two-week cycles, each ending in a working demo |
| **Launch** | 1–2 weeks | Load testing, launch checklist and deployment |
| **Grow** | Ongoing | Monitoring, support and the next improvements |

## Why work with us

- **Builders you can reach:** you talk to the engineers writing the code.
- **Honest scope:** we estimate after discovery, and you approve changes first.
- **You keep ownership:** source code, credentials and documentation are yours.
- **Security from the start:** role-based access, encryption and audit logging are built in early.
- **Support after launch:** every project includes a support window.
