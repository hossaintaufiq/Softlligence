# Surface brief: homepage

Mode: Persuade. Scope: homepage, shared nav and footer, light/dark design tokens (inner pages inherit tokens only).
Direction pinned by the user's brief (Stripe/Linear/Vercel/Framer quality; navy, electric blue, indigo, restrained violet; light and dark). Replaces the earlier Multi-Part Form Set direction.
Audience/job: ops and IT leaders choosing software; action is Explore Our Products or Let's Talk.
Proof/content: real product records, client projects, industries, FAQ, contact. Product previews are illustrative and labeled Sample data. Stat counters come from the site's existing homeTrustStats and still need owner verification.
Unresolved: verified stats; whether MES/BI become packaged products; no /solutions or /resources pages exist (nav dropdowns link to existing pages).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A SaaS product company page whose hero is a live-looking product interface, not an agency banner. It refuses the centered-headline-over-icon-cards template.
OWN-WORLD: Deep navy band sections against a cool off-white (light) or near-black navy (dark); electric blue to indigo gradient reserved for primary actions and the active chart bar; violet only as a restrained wash. Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist body. Mock interfaces in framed windows with a Sample data tag. Bento, tabbed showcase and editorial list layouts, never repeated equal cards.
STORY: A buyer sees an intelligent software company with real products, picks a product from the tabbed showcase, sees the custom-enterprise range, the industries, why the team is accountable, and talks to the company.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: four-line headline, supporting paragraph, Explore Our Products and Let's Talk. Right: tilted ERP dashboard with an AI recommendation panel and CRM pipeline panel at different depths. Centered nav with theme switcher and Let's talk at the right.
FORM: Pinned by the user brief, not rolled. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
